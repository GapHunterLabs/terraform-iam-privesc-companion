Reports a Terraform aws_iam_role that can reach an admin-equivalent role through a chain of AssumeRole trust relationships defined in the project.

Each step may look harmless on its own, but the chain is a privilege-escalation path (CWE-269). All .tf files of the project are read into a graph of trust edges and policy grants, and a breadth-first search finds the nearest admin-equivalent role, before terraform apply runs.
